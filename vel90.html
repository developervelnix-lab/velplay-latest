Why would a withdrawal be held?

Almost always an incomplete verification file, or a bonus that still has wagering left on it. Both show as a reason on the transaction, and support can clear the first one in minutes.

Can I cancel a pending withdrawal?

You can, but we'd rather you didn't — reversing a payout back into a playable balance is a common way people lose money they'd already decided to take out. The option is in your cashier if you need it.

Is there a fee?

No fee from us on any method. Crypto networks charge their own miner fee, which is shown before you confirm.
